Wolverine Habitat Suitability.

A terrain and visibility analysis exploring how suitable habitat can be evaluated from selected viewpoints.

process

I applied suitability criteria to environmental raster data, reclassified the inputs, and used Raster Calculator to identify potential high-quality denning areas. I then compared the suitable areas with terrain-based visibility from two viewpoints.

methods

Raster reclassification, Raster Calculator, suitability analysis, viewshed analysis, terrain visualization, and cartographic design.

tools

ArcGIS Pro · raster analysis · suitability modeling · viewshed analysis · cartography
